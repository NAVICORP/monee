Questions

Good to know.

Is Pursee free?

Yes. Pursee is free and has no ads, for now.

Where is my data kept?

On SkiFi's own server. Your book stays in step across your phone, tablet and computer. We never sell it.

Can I move from Money Manager?

Yes. Export its Excel backup, then open More, then Import in Pursee and pick the file.

Which currencies can I use?

Any. Pick one main currency for your totals and give each account its own. The rate is kept with each entry, and you can change it.

Can my family see it?

If you want. Share the book and choose who can edit it and who can only view it.

Can I use it on my computer?

Yes. Open monee.skifi.co/app in any browser and sign in. It is the same book as on your phone.
