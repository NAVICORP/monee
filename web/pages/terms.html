The short version of using Monee. Last updated 3 November 2026.

What Monee is

Monee is a money manager. It keeps a record of your income, expenses, accounts, cards and budgets. It does not move money, connect to banks or give financial advice.

Your books

Your books belong to you. You are responsible for what you enter and for the people you share a book with.

Exchange rates

Totals in your main currency use the rate kept with each entry. Rates can be out of date, so check them before you rely on a total. You can change any rate.

Accounts and sharing

Keep your sign-in to yourself. When you share a book, the people you add can see it, and editors can change it.

Price

Monee is free and has no ads, for now. If paid features come later, they will be clearly marked in the app.

Fair use

Do not use Monee to break the law or to try to reach another person's book.

No guarantee

We work to keep Monee running and your books safe, with nightly copies, but we cannot promise it will never be down or never lose a change. Monee is provided as it is.
