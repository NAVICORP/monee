Support

Help with Pursee. Email contact@skifi.co and we will reply.

I cannot sign in

Pursee uses the SkiFi login. Sign in with the same email or phone number you used before. It is the same login as other SkiFi apps.

Starting out

Sign in, pick your main currency, then add your accounts. Starter groups and categories are ready, and you can change them in More.

Moving from Money Manager

In Money Manager, make an Excel backup. In Pursee, open More, then Import, and pick the file.

Using Pursee on a computer

Open monee.skifi.co/app in any browser and sign in. It is the same book as on your phone.

Sharing a book with family

Add people as editors or viewers. Editors can add and change entries. Viewers can only look.
